## Developer and ecosystem newsletter

Our [Developer and ecosystem](https://microbit.us14.list-manage.com/subscribe?u=e1c30f24b90ff3d70275cfff2&id=25403c7650) newsletter is a low volume digest on the technical aspects of micro:bit, in addition to the regular [Micro:bit Educational Foundation newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/microbit/newsletter).
